LOCATION UPDATE
- School Profile now contains all 36 Nigerian states plus the Federal Capital Territory (FCT).
- Local Government Area is a separate dependent field.
- Selecting a state automatically loads only its LGAs.
- FCT loads its six Area Councils.
- Embedded locally for offline demo use; no internet/API is required.

ACTION PLAN UPDATE
- Local Government Area now appears in the downloaded school plan.
- The downloaded plan uses clearer, more natural school details.
- Internet and printing access are described in readable language.
- Readiness now displays "Not yet assessed" instead of an awkward percentage when no assessment exists.
- Recommendations are framed as practical school next steps.


UPDATE — STATE-AWARE READINESS + LONG-DISRUPTION CONTINUITY

1. Continuity Mode now handles 2-week, 1-month, 3-month and 5-month disruption periods.
   Existing learner data is only a starting baseline; later learning cycles use new checks and curriculum progression.

2. Readiness Check now adapts 4 of 10 questions to the saved State/LGA context.
   Oyo, Lagos, Bayelsa, Borno, Adamawa, Yobe, Kaduna, Katsina, Zamfara and Niger have specific planning prompts.
   Other states receive state/LGA-contextual planning prompts automatically.
   These are education-continuity planning prompts, not official risk assessments.

3. Print Readiness Report now includes:
   - School name
   - State
   - Local Government Area (LGA)
   - School level
   - Estimated learners
   - Readiness score
   - Every readiness question with YES/NO status
